Legal Authorities Compliance

CATE complies with all relevant child safety laws, and reports to regional and national authorities, including NCMEC and law enforcement.

1 Strict 18+ Age Requirement & Verification

CATE is an exclusive dating and professional networking application strictly designed for adults aged 18 and older. Minors are explicitly prohibited from registering, creating profiles, or accessing any features within the application.

  • Age Gating: All registration flows require explicit birthdate input and mandatory 18+ identity confirmation.
  • Account Termination: Any profile found or suspected to belong to a minor under 18 years of age is immediately suspended and permanently banned.
  • Identity Moderation: Automated and human verification mechanisms check uploaded photographs and profile details for age compliance.

2 Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

CATE maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit the creation, upload, storage, solicitation, or distribution of any content that exploits or endangers children.

Any user attempt to produce, transmit, share, or solicit CSAM/CSAE will result in instant permanent account termination, device-level banning, IP blocking, and immediate legal escalation to law enforcement.

3 In-App Safety & Reporting Mechanisms

CATE provides built-in, easy-to-use reporting tools directly accessible within the mobile app interface:

  • Profile Reporting: Users can flag any user profile card immediately by selecting "Report User" and choosing child safety/harassment categories.
  • Chat Conversation Reporting: Within active messages, users can tap conversation settings to report individual messages or entire interaction threads.
  • Priority Review: Reports marked under child safety or exploitation are routed directly to our highest-priority emergency moderation queue.

4 Content Moderation & Automated Safeguards

To ensure strict safety standards, CATE utilizes a multi-layered security infrastructure:

  • Automated Screening: Images uploaded to CATE servers are screened against known hashes and automated classification systems designed to detect prohibited content.
  • Keyword & Behavior Analysis: Text interactions are analyzed using automated threat detection to identify predatory language, grooming behaviors, or illegal solicitation.
  • Dedicated Safety Operations: Moderation specialists review flagged content and take swift protective measures round-the-clock.

5 Reporting to Law Enforcement & Regional Authorities

CATE complies with all regional, national, and international child protection legislation. In accordance with statutory obligations:

  • NCMEC Submissions: Any instance of suspected CSAM or CSAE identified on our platform is reported immediately to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.
  • Global Law Enforcement Cooperation: We report instances to appropriate international bodies (such as INHOPE networks, Interpol, and local law enforcement authorities).
  • Data Preservation: CATE preserves relevant electronic evidence, metadata, logs, and account records as permitted by law to assist criminal investigations and prosecutions.
